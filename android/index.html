ScreenFleet Player

The ScreenFleet app for Android TV boxes and sticks.

Download ScreenFleet Player 2.1.0
  1. Open the downloaded file and choose Install (allow installs from this app if asked).
  2. Open ScreenFleet — a setup code and QR code appear on your TV.
  3. Scan the QR code with your phone and follow the steps.
