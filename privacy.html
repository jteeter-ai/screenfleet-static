ScreenFleet Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026 · Applies to app.screenfleet.io and the ScreenFleet Player apps (Android, Amazon Signage Stick, BrightSign, Windows and web)

ScreenFleet is a digital signage service operated by Legion LED Trucks, LLC ("ScreenFleet", "we", "us"). Businesses use ScreenFleet to manage and schedule content on their screens. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When a business signs up or a user is invited, we collect name, email address, organization name, role, time zone and sign-in activity. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.

Content you upload. Images, videos, playlists, schedules and other materials that customers upload to display on their screens.

Player device information. A ScreenFleet Player installed on a TV stick, media player or computer sends:

  • a random device identifier created by the app, and the activation code shown on screen;
  • device make and model, operating system version, app version and screen resolution;
  • network status (connection type, local network address, online/offline) and check-in times;
  • playback records (which content played, when and on which screen) — "proof of play";
  • a screenshot of what the screen is showing, only when an authorized user of the customer's account requests one.

The Player does not use the device's camera or microphone, does not collect location from GPS, contacts or other apps, and contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

2. How we use information

  • to provide the service: pair players to accounts, deliver and schedule content, and show screen status;
  • to produce proof-of-play reports for the customer;
  • to troubleshoot, secure and improve ScreenFleet;
  • to bill customers and communicate with them about their account.

We do not sell personal information.

3. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run ScreenFleet (for example cloud hosting, the Base44 application platform, and Stripe for payments), when required by law, or as part of a business transfer. Customer content and player data are visible to the authorized users of that customer's ScreenFleet account.

4. Retention

We keep account and player information while the account is active. When a player is unpaired or an account is closed, we delete or anonymize the related information within a reasonable period unless we must keep it for legal, billing or security reasons.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access is restricted to authorized users of each customer organization.

6. Your choices

Customers can unpair a player at any time from the ScreenFleet dashboard or the player's on-screen menu, which stops it sending information. To access, correct or delete information, contact us at the address below.

7. Children

ScreenFleet is a business service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
